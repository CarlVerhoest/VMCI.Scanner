name: reproducing-is-not-proving
description: A green golden test or a perfect reconciliation proves you copied the reference, not that the reference was right

---

**Matching a reference document proves transcription, not correctness.** In eTrustee a calculation
reconciled 131 of 131 lines against a filed claim and its golden test was green — and the figure was
1.440 EUR too high, because the reference itself contained an item that must never be included. It
surfaced only through an unrelated rule from the expert.

**How to apply:** treat "the numbers match" as evidence you read the document correctly, nothing
more. Ask "is this the complete list of what exists here?", not only "which reading is right?". When
a golden test must move because a person overruled the reference, keep the old figure and the gap
visible in the test, so it reads as a decision rather than drift.
